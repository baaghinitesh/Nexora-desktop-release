## What's New in v1.0.9

### 1. Fluid Drag-and-Drop App Launcher & Quick Launch Reordering
* **Long-Press & Drag**: Hold any app shortcut in either the Launcher or the Keyboard Quick Launch bar to reorder effortlessly with smooth haptic feedback and live slot previews.
* **Bi-Directional Order Sync**: Persistent launcher customization automatically reflected across both the full App Launcher and Quick Launch bars.
* **Permanent Defaults**: Pre-configured priority shortcuts (Launch Your Concept & Flash Tools) always stay reliably available and customizable.
* **Play Store Version Code 11**: Updated build targeting Google Play Store requirements with production keystore signature.

### 2. Unified Nexora Branded Dialog System
* **Consistent Modal Experience**: Replaced raw platform alerts with polished, unified Nexora-branded dialogs across permission prompts, sync choices, clipboard alerts, deletion confirmations, and force updates.
* **Modern Acrylic Aesthetics**: Dark theme styling, high-contrast action buttons, and prominent Nexora app identity branding.

### 3. Smart Trackpad with Native Drag-Selection
* **Double-Tap-and-Drag**: Double-tap and hold on the trackpad surface to highlight text, select code blocks, and move Windows app windows seamlessly.
* **Ultra-Low Latency (<5ms)**: UDP datagram pipeline ensures instantaneous cursor tracking.
* **Multi-Touch Gestures**: Two-finger fluid scrolling, pinch-to-zoom, two-finger right-click, three-finger middle-click, and infinite scrollbar.

### 4. Live Pen Tablet & Quick Screen Navigation
* **Transparent Desktop Overlay**: Strokes drawn on your mobile screen are mirrored live onto a transparent fullscreen Windows Ghost Window overlay.
* **Quick Navigation Icons**: Jump directly between Pen Tablet, Touchpad, Keyboard, and App Launcher from the streamlined top navigation bar.

### 5. Advanced PC Keyboard & Media Hub
* **Full Unicode & Emoji Typing**: Real-time C# `SendUnicodeString` key injection allows typing all characters and emojis directly into any Windows app.
* **Media & Navigation Controls**: Play/Pause, Next/Previous Track, Mute, Volume adjustments, F1–F12 keys, and essential Windows hotkeys (Win+L, Win+R, Ctrl+Shift+Esc, PrtSc, Win+Shift+S).

### 6. Wireless File Transfers & Clipboard Sync
* **High-Speed Transfers**: Chunked local streaming up to 40+ MB/s directly into your PC Downloads directory.
* **Real-Time Clipboard Mirroring**: Zero-click clipboard text sync between phone and computer.

## Installation Guide

1. Download **`Nexora-Mobile.apk`** on your Android phone using the link above or update via Google Play.
2. If installing the APK directly, tap the downloaded file to install (allow "Install from Unknown Sources" in Android settings if prompted).
3. Open **Nexora** on your phone.
4. Ensure both your PC and phone are connected to the same Wi-Fi router (or PC mobile hotspot).
5. Tap **Connect** and scan the QR code on your PC screen!
