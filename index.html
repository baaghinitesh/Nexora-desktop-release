Setup Instructions

💻 Setting up Desktop App

  • 1

    Download: Click the button above to get the Nexora-Setup.exe file.

  • 2

    Install: Open the installer and follow the setup guide. Select standard installation options.

  • 3

    Firewall Config: The installer will automatically add rules to Windows Firewall to permit local connections.

  • 4

    Run: Start Nexora. It will generate a connection QR code and list your computer's local IP address.

📱 Setting up Mobile App

  • 1

    Download: Click the green button above on your Android phone to download the Nexora-Mobile.apk file.

  • 2

    Install: Open the downloaded APK. If prompted, enable "Install from Unknown Sources" for your file manager.

  • 3

    Connect: Ensure both your PC and phone are on the same Wi-Fi network (or hotspot).

  • 4

    Pair: Open Nexora Mobile, click Connect, and scan the QR code on your computer's monitor. Done!
